Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What we collect

HifzLink processes the following information to provide the service:

  • Usage analytics — If analytics is enabled for this deployment, a self-hosted Umami instance records page views and referrers for aggregate usage reports. The analytics server's configuration determines what technical request metadata it retains.
  • Account and session data (optional) — When you sign in with Google, HifzLink validates Google's signed ID token and receives your Google account identifier, verified email address, and profile name. It stores these details and a HifzLink session in its server-side database to maintain your login and associate collections with your account. Google OAuth tokens are used only during sign-in and are not stored.
  • Saved items — Collection names, descriptions, ayah and pair references, notes, and review status are stored in HifzLink's server-side database.
  • Operational logs — The server logs the HTTP method and request path for requests. Log storage and retention depend on the deployment.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers.
  • HifzLink does not receive or store your Google password or Google OAuth tokens. Google handles authentication.

Third-party services

  • Google — Sign-in uses Google OAuth to verify your account and retrieve your profile name and email. Google handles the authentication flow under its own policies.
  • Quran Foundation — The Content API may provide verse audio. It is not used to sign in users or to sync bookmarks.
  • Google Fonts — Fonts load from Google's CDN, so your browser sends a request to Google when a page loads. Google may log request metadata under its own policies.
  • Umami analytics — If enabled, analytics are sent to the self-hosted Umami service configured for the deployment.
  • Tanzil — The Quran text is bundled from Tanzil. HifzLink does not send data to Tanzil at runtime.

Cookies

Signing in sets an HTTP-only session cookie that lasts up to 30 days. It is marked secure on HTTPS deployments. Logging out removes the session. Temporary cookies protect the Google sign-in flow. HifzLink does not set advertising cookies.

Data retention

Expired session records are deleted when the application starts; logging out deletes the current session immediately. The admin dashboard can display account email/name, sign-in time, session status, and collection/item totals. It does not expose saved notes or verse content. Saved collection data remains in HifzLink's database until deletion is requested. You can remove individual saved items in the app, but the current interface does not delete an entire collection or all account data. Contact us to request that deletion.

Contact

Questions or data deletion requests: mail@srmdn.com
