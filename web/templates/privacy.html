Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

What we collect

HifzLink processes the following information to provide the service:

  • Usage analytics — If analytics is enabled for this deployment, a self-hosted Umami instance records page views and referrers for aggregate usage reports. The analytics server's configuration determines what technical request metadata it retains.
  • Account and session data (optional) — When you sign in through Quran Foundation, HifzLink receives your account identifier, name, email address, and OAuth access and refresh tokens. The app stores these in its server-side database to maintain your session, associate your collections with your account, and access Quran Foundation bookmark features.
  • Saved items — Collection names, descriptions, ayah and pair references, notes, and review status are stored in HifzLink's server-side database.
  • Operational logs — The server logs the HTTP method and request path for requests. Log storage and retention depend on the deployment.

What we do not collect

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers.
  • HifzLink does not receive or store your Quran Foundation password. Authentication is handled by Quran Foundation.

Third-party services

  • Quran Foundation — Sign-in uses its OAuth service. When bookmark integration is enabled, HifzLink reads your Quran Foundation bookmarks for the dashboard and adds the primary ayah from a newly saved item as a bookmark in your Quran Foundation account. HifzLink collections themselves are stored by HifzLink. Quran Foundation's policies apply to information it processes.
  • Google Fonts — Fonts load from Google's CDN, so your browser sends a request to Google when a page loads. Google may log request metadata under its own policies.
  • Umami analytics — If enabled, analytics are sent to the self-hosted Umami service configured for the deployment.
  • Tanzil — The Quran text is bundled from Tanzil. HifzLink does not send data to Tanzil at runtime.

Cookies

Signing in sets a secure, HTTP-only session cookie that lasts up to 30 days and may be renewed when an access token is refreshed. Logging out removes the session. Temporary cookies are also used during the Quran Foundation sign-in flow. HifzLink does not set advertising cookies.

Data retention

Expired session records are deleted when the application starts; logging out deletes the current session immediately. Saved collection data remains in HifzLink's database until deletion is requested. You can remove individual saved items in the app, but the current interface does not delete an entire collection or all account data. Contact us to request that deletion.

Contact

Questions or data deletion requests: mail@srmdn.com
